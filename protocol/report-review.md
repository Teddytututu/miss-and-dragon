# Reports refined by the research lead

[简体中文](report-review.zh-CN.md)

The executor prepares a report, figures, source-backed numbers and a separate technical record. Submit the artifact in the existing lead exchange for review. The lead verifies the evidence, interpretation and reader's needs, then refines the report before delivery to the owner. This applies to result pages, tables with a narrative, study reports and research summaries.

Keep the draft link and review request in the assistant exchange. The lead decides when the refined report is ready, who delivers it and whether it gets a user-action card. The assigned deliverer checks the original conversation before posting the single completion reply. The owner's explicit instructions can set a different workflow.

## What the review covers

- Put the strongest supported answer first, with the relevant comparison and denominator.
- Distinguish evidence sources, controlled comparisons, observations and hypotheses.
- Replace completed estimates with the actual result and explain unfamiliar scientific terms.
- Keep numerical results, authorship and claim strength stable while refining the narrative.
- Open the rendered artifact and check its figures, formulas and links.

## Lead refinement record template

After each review, append reusable lessons to the project's local report-writing guidance and apply them to the next report. Keep these editorial records beside the working material; the reader-facing report contains its scientific content.

```markdown
## Lead refinements

### Reader question or report topic

- Reader's question:
- Passage or figure refined:
- Approved wording or structure:
- Evidence and numerical facts preserved:
- General rule for the next report:
```

Link the local guidance from the project's reporting instructions or report skill. Use project-relative artifact paths in the review exchange. Public source packages contain reusable rules and blank templates; personal review records stay in the local project.
