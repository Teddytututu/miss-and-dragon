## English

Miss & Dragon keeps a person in the loop through the board, discussion room and private messages. The lead assistant (Claude) defines tasks and accepts results; the executor (GPT) implements authorized work. The night watch observes configured progress, updates counters and estimates, and records deduplicated event IDs. Assistants read events, handle authorized follow-up and acknowledge completed conditions. Lifecycle and stability checks route issues back for review and repair.

## 中文

Miss & Dragon 通过看板、讨论室与私聊让使用者参与回路。主线助手 Claude 定义任务并验收成果，执行助手 GPT 完成已授权工作。守夜人观察配置的进度，更新计数与预计时间，记录去重事件。助手读取事件、处理授权范围内的后续工作，再确认事件。流程与稳定性检查把问题送回复核与修正。
