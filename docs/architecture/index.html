People, chats and persistent records

Chats make decisions and deliver work; programs retain records, observe state and deliver events. Solid arrows write records; dashed arrows read them.

02

The communication channels behind the board

Exchange notes retain discussion and decisions; the decision ledger retains execution choices and overrides. Separate private chats connect the owner to each AI role.

Visibility describes the usual entry point: the board shows conversations, files retain their history, and program channels carry events and progress.

03

A question travels through review and delivery

Read the numbers in time order: the question enters the inbox and design review, then becomes an executable task and job. Accepted results enter the findings and result tree.

Time labels describe cadence. Experiment and review duration depend on the task.

04

Daily cadence: model chats and ordinary programs

Purple and orange lanes are model chats; teal lanes are ordinary programs. Events and fallback schedules keep the workflow moving.

Guardian checks local services; Night watch observes tasks and records events. Status checks the chat model, and the lead reviews the audit twice daily.

05

Decision authority and the audit loop

The owner sets direction and authorization; the lead owns design and acceptance; executors implement and record routine choices. Five consequential categories enter the ask channel.

The audit turns waste into engineering changes and the next review checks the result. Numbered questions and veto records preserve the decision history.

06

The wake loop finishes actionable work

After handling events, experiment checks available resources; every role then checks lead orders, open tasks and returned work. Continue the original task until a real dependency remains.

The closing patrol records current work, the next task, the dependency and the assistant’s mood.
